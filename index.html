About

I'm an MBA candidate at UC Berkeley's Haas School of Business (Class of 2028), coming from six years in aerospace R&D and business development at Continuum Dynamics, an R&D firm focused on rotorcraft, fixed-wing, and advanced air mobility (AAM) aeromechanics. There, I captured $1.44M in DoD and NASA awards, led cross-organization technical teams, and opened new commercial revenue in the emerging AAM market.

My path started in engineering: a Ph.D. and M.S.E. in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Michigan, where my dissertation on simulating helicopter shipboard landings was published in the Journal of Aircraft, and a B.S.E. in Mechanical Engineering (with a Math minor) from the University of Iowa. That technical foundation is what let me translate deep-tech capability into funded programs and paying customers — and it's the lens I bring to business strategy at Haas.
